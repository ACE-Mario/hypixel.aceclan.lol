Hover a perk to see what it does, click it to change it. Like in game: button = not unlocked, planks = levelled, log = maxed. ▲ / ▼ means the plan raises or lowers it, red means it's locked or not connected, and your selected ability shimmers.

What to do

Whispers you own are what you'd have after a reset: your unspent whispers plus what's in your perks. Center of the Forest isn't counted, because a reset never gives it back. They fill in from your profile.
