Break any SkyBlock item down to what you have to farm yourself.

New custom item

Group several items into one target, for example a full armor set or every item for a build. The planner adds up everything they need.

What are you working towards?

Search for an item above. The planner pulls its recipe, then the recipes of every ingredient, until it reaches things you have to gather yourself, like drops, ores, crops and fragments. On Ironman that list is the grind.

  • Raw materials: a checklist of everything to farm. Enter how many you have and watch it fill.
  • Recipe tree: every craft, forge and NPC purchase in the chain. Already own an intermediate? Enter it and its branch disappears.
  • Requirements: the collections, slayers and HotM tiers you need to unlock along the way.
  • Steps: the crafts in order, so you can tick them off.
